# WinSpeed user guide

WinSpeed is a free, open-source internet speed meter for Windows 10 and 11. It sits on the taskbar next to the clock and shows your live download and upload speed. It also keeps a data-usage history, runs speed tests and warns you before you hit a data cap.

![Taskbar meter](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/anand34577/winspeed/main/docs/images/taskbar-meter.png)
