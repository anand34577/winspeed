Features

Everything a speed meter should do.
Nothing it shouldn't.

Small enough to forget it's running, thorough enough to answer "why is my internet slow?"

Live taskbar meter

Download and upload speed next to the clock, stacked or on one line, with an optional mini graph and ping.

Built-in speed test

Download, upload, ping and jitter on Cloudflare's global network, with history, CSV export and scheduling.

Data usage history

Hour by hour and per adapter. View by day, month or year, drill down, and export to a spreadsheet.

Data-cap alerts

Set a monthly or daily limit and your billing day. WinSpeed warns you at your chosen percentage and at 100 %.

Connection monitor

Latency, jitter and packet loss, plus a notification the moment your internet drops and when it's back.

Private by design

No telemetry, no ads, no account. Privacy mode masks IP and MAC addresses when you share your screen.

Make it yours

Your units. Your look. Your spot.

Every option applies instantly, so you can see exactly what you're getting.

  • KB/s or Mbps: automatic bytes or bits, or any fixed unit, with a 1024 or 1000 multiplier
  • Labels, fonts and colours: arrows, text, background opacity and speed highlights
  • Anywhere: next to the tray, on the left, or as a floating widget; just drag it
  • Out of the way: hides during full-screen games and videos, or click straight through it

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is WinSpeed really free?

Yes. It's open source under the MIT licence: free for personal and commercial use, with no ads, no premium tier and no account.

Does it work on Windows 11's centred taskbar?

Yes. It sits next to the system tray on Windows 11 and Windows 10, handles vertical and auto-hiding taskbars, and can also run as a floating widget.

Will it slow my PC down?

No. It uses about 20 MB of memory and well under 1 % CPU. The window only uses resources while it's open.

Why does Windows SmartScreen warn me?

The installer isn't code-signed yet. Click More info → Run anyway. Every release is built publicly by GitHub Actions and comes with SHA-256 checksums.

What does it send over the internet?

Nothing about you. A small ping checks your latency (you can turn it off), it checks GitHub for updates once a day (optional), and the speed test and public-IP lookup only run when you ask. Details.

My numbers double when I use a VPN. Why?

They won't by default. WinSpeed counts only physical adapters, because a VPN sends the same traffic through two adapters. You can change which adapters are counted in Settings.
